Hi, I'm Kanghyeok Im. I use maps and data to tackle urban problems.

  1. 01

    2015 – 2020 · Electronic Engineering, University of Suwon

    From circuits to cities

    I started out in electronic engineering. Over time I wanted to work on something broader than a single technology — something that improves the lives of many people. I found that answer in the city, where people live, work, and spend their days, so I decided to change my field.

  2. 02

    2020 – 2023 · Chung-Ang University

    Discovering urban studies

    I transferred into Urban Systems Engineering at Chung-Ang University and met urban studies for the first time. Learning how to read, plan, and design cities drew me in deeper, and I graduated first in my department with an award-winning capstone project.

  3. 03

    2023 – 2025 · Graduate School of Environmental Studies, Seoul National University

    Asking cities questions with data

    At Seoul National University I focused on GIS and spatial data analysis. My master's thesis asked, “Where do people who leave depopulating regions go, and what work do they find?” — and won the Best Paper Award from the Korean Planning Association. I also worked on commissioned studies for K-water on water demand management and on Korean water culture, serving as project manager for the latter.

  4. 04

    2026 – Present · Gyeonggi Research Institute

    Now, at the front line of the energy transition

    I work in the Department of Climate, Energy & Environment at Gyeonggi Research Institute. I use data and spatial analysis to understand what cities need to prepare on the road to carbon neutrality, and how the energy transition will reshape them.
